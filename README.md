#Overview
f(R) Cosmology Model is an alternate way to explain the composition of the universe. In the traditional Lambda Cold Dark Matter model, there is a component named as dark matter. We don't know much about dark energy because scientists added it only because the theoretical hypothesis from Einstein's General Theory of Relativity didn't match with the experimental results when rotation curves of galaxies were studied. To compensate for the gap between theory and experiment, this mysterious dark energy term was added without any explanation. In the f(R) gravity model, we aim to explain the expansion of the universe by altering the geometry of spacetime rather than banking on something we don't know much about. In this model we'll take the curvature of spacetime to be some function of R (The Ricci Scalar) rather than just R in the Einstein's Theory of Relativity.

#Numerical Analysis
Two methods of Numerical Analysis have been used namely Finite Difference Method and the Chebyshev Spectral Method and both of which have been compared to give the best results, later we are going to use MCMC and Log Likelihood methods to interpret the best possible values for the cosmological parameters.
